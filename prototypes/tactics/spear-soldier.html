Spear Soldier

Chibi pixel art in the style of your lumberjack, placed by hand pixel by pixel, with a full set of animations. The red one is the same sprite with a different palette.

Every animation

The sprite sheet

One row per animation, one 76 × 48 box per frame. This is the file a game would load.

How it's made: the head, body, arms, legs, spear tips and fists are drawn pixel by pixel. Each frame places those parts, and a pass adds the dark outline around the whole figure. New units, like an archer or a knight, mostly mean redrawing a few parts and picking a palette.
