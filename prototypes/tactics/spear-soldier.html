Spear Soldier

Built to your lumberjack's proportions: a big head, broad shoulders, oversized fists, thick legs and chunky boots, with soft shading on every surface and a full set of animations. The red one is the same soldier in enemy colours.

The sprite sheet

One row per animation, one 164 × 112 box per frame. These PNGs are the files a game would load.

How it's made: the soldier is built from shaped, shaded parts (helmet, head, padded tunic, fists, legs, boots, spear) that are posed for each frame, turned into pixels with a dark outline, and given a hand-placed face. New units, like an archer or a knight, mean new parts and colours on the same skeleton.
