Magnus and Dio, sparring

Ogre Battle 64's Magnus and Dio, redrawn from a field screenshot. Every pixel is computed by code on this page: the village road is ray-traced when the page opens, both fighters are drawn from shapes on a skeleton, and the final picture goes through the N64's 320 × 240 screen, 16-bit colour and soft video filter.

The two fighters up close

  • OB64 proportions. Big heads, broad shoulders and short, thick limbs, about four and a half heads tall, instead of Rowan's slimmer build.
  • Seen the way the game shows them. Your side's unit is drawn from behind and the opponent from the front, as on OB64's field screens.
  • A matching field. A cobbled village road with a signpost, hedges, a dry-stone wall and a fence, ray-traced with sun shadows and sky light, then run through the N64 filter.

In the field

At N64 resolution each fighter is about 55 to 75 pixels tall, depending on how far back he stands.

How a frame is made

The attack, frame by frame

Runs at 16 frames per second. Like OB64's melee units, Magnus runs up to the target, strikes and hops back into formation.
