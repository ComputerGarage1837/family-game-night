Three passes at the same knight

  • What makes it read as N64. Sprites stand in a perspective scene and get smaller with distance, and the console smooths them when it scales them. OB64's own sprites were noted as a little blurry for this reason.
  • A pre-rendered battlefield. OB64 fought on pre-rendered backgrounds. This one is ray-traced once when the page opens: sun shadows, sky light, ambient occlusion and haze.
  • The console's screen. The final frame is 320 × 240, reduced to 16-bit colour with the N64's ordered dither, then softened sideways the way its video output did. Turn the filter off above to compare.
