Rowan Knight · Lv 12

An original character in the spirit of Ogre Battle 64's battle sprites. Every pixel is computed by code on this page: no image files, no drawing app. The knight fights a training dummy on a loop, the way an idle game plays itself.

At game size

The sprite lives in a 96 × 96 pixel frame. On a battle screen it would sit around 1× to 2×; the big stage above is 4× or more.

How it's drawn

Because a frame is generated from a pose, a new animation is a handful of numbers, and a palette swap re-colours every frame at once.

The attack, frame by frame

Runs at 16 frames per second. Like OB64's melee units, Rowan runs up to the target, strikes and hops back into formation.
